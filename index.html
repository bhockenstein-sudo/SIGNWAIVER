The agreement

What we do out there

True Wild Coastal runs private marine expeditions on Howe Sound and the surrounding coastal waters from Squamish, BC. Activities may include wildlife and whale watching, photography, fishing charters, freediving, spearfishing, and kayaking, aboard a 20-foot welded aluminum centre console vessel in open coastal waters.

Assumption of risk

I understand that being on the water carries inherent risks, dangers, and hazards that cannot be eliminated, including:

  • Rough, unpredictable, or rapidly changing sea and weather conditions
  • Capsizing, swamping, or falling overboard
  • Contact with marine wildlife such as whales, seals, and sea lions
  • Hypothermia, cold water exposure, and drowning
  • Injury from vessel motion, equipment, or gear
  • Remote locations with limited access to emergency services
  • Risks specific to freediving, spearfishing, or water entry, where applicable
  • Equipment failure or malfunction

I freely and voluntarily accept and assume all such risks, both known and unknown, arising from my participation.

Release of liability and waiver of claims

In consideration of being permitted to participate, I, on behalf of myself, my heirs, executors, administrators, and assigns, release, waive, discharge, and agree not to sue True Wild Coastal, its owners, guides, crew, employees, agents, and contractors (the "Released Parties") from any and all liability, claims, demands, or causes of action for any loss, damage, injury, or death sustained while participating, whether caused by the negligence of the Released Parties or otherwise. I understand this release includes claims based on negligence and that I am giving up substantial legal rights by agreeing.

Indemnification

I agree to indemnify and hold harmless the Released Parties from any loss, liability, damage, cost, or expense, including legal fees, they may incur due to my participation, including claims brought by third parties arising from my conduct.

My responsibilities

  • Follow all safety instructions and directions from the guide at all times
  • Wear a personal flotation device when instructed
  • Disclose any medical conditions, allergies, medications, or limitations that may affect my participation
  • Behave responsibly toward myself, other participants, wildlife, and the vessel
  • Comply with all federal and provincial regulations, including Transport Canada requirements and the Species at Risk Act

Governing law

This agreement is governed by the laws of British Columbia and the federal laws of Canada. Any dispute is subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of British Columbia.
